Straight answers

Will it make things up?

It answers only from the documents you give us, and it is built to say "I will pass you to a colleague" instead of guessing. Every answer records which document it came from, so you can check any conversation. For anything sensitive — medical, legal, money — it always hands off to a person.

Is our customer data used to train AI?

No. We use business API plans that do not train on your data, we store only what the automation needs, and we delete everything if you leave. That is written into the contract.

What if it gets something wrong?

Before launch it must pass a written list of real questions from your business — you approve that list. After launch, fixes are included in your monthly plan, and we watch the logs so we usually spot problems before you do.

Do we need to change our systems?

Almost never. It works with the calendar, inbox, CRM and website you already use. If you use paper for something, we will tell you honestly whether automating it is worth it yet.

What does it really cost to run?

The monthly plan covers hosting, monitoring and support. Phone minutes and heavy usage are billed at cost plus a small margin, with a monthly allowance agreed in advance — so there are no surprise bills.

How fast can we start?

Most builds go live in one to three weeks. A demo on your own content usually takes us a day or two.
