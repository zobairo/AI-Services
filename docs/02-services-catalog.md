# Services Catalog — The Full Menu

Every service you can charge for: **52 services in six categories**, plus **12 industry packages** ([08](08-industry-packages.md)). Each entry: what it is (in client language), who buys it, and price. Prices show **starting → established** (first ~3 months vs. after reputation); retainer logic and tiers are in [Pricing](01-business-model-and-pricing.md).

**You do not launch all of this at once.** Phase 1 sells A1 + D1 (see "rollout order" at the end). The rest switches on category by category through [the scaling roadmap](07-scaling-roadmap.md). Two services done excellently beat fifty listed — the catalog exists so every client conversation has a next upsell, and so no revenue is left on the table as you grow.

## Category A — Customer-facing AI (they talk to your client's customers)

**A1. AI support chatbot.** Answers customer questions on the website, WhatsApp, Telegram, or Instagram DMs using the client's own docs and policies (RAG), hands off to a human when unsure. *Buyers: e-commerce, clinics, any service business.* The flagship entry product — fastest demo, clearest ROI.

**A2. AI voice receptionist.** Picks up the phone 24/7: answers FAQs, takes bookings, qualifies callers, sends the summary to the client's CRM/WhatsApp. *Buyers: clinics, salons, restaurants, real-estate, law firms.* Voice minutes billed as usage ([stack](03-tech-stack-and-architecture.md)); a missed call is a lost customer, so this sells itself once demoed.

**A3. AI outbound voice.** Calls leads back within a minute of a form submission, confirms appointments the day before, revives no-shows. Always with clear AI disclosure and opt-out. *Buyers: clinics, real-estate, driving schools, booking businesses.*

**A4. AI sales assistant / product finder.** A guided "what do you need?" conversation that recommends products, answers comparison questions, and pushes to checkout or books a sales call. *Buyers: e-commerce with big catalogs, B2B with configurable services.*

**A5. Appointment booking agent.** Chat or voice flow connected to the real calendar: books, reschedules, reminds, fills cancellations from a waitlist. *Buyers: clinics, salons, consultants, workshops.*

**A6. Multilingual add-on.** Any A-service answering in the customer's language (e.g. Arabic + English + French) with tone control per language. +30–50% on the base service — high perceived value, low extra cost for you.

**A7. Review & reputation automation.** Drafts replies to every Google/Trustpilot review in the right tone, flags angry ones to a human first, requests reviews from happy customers after purchase/visit. *Buyers: restaurants, clinics, hotels, local services.*

**A8. WhatsApp commerce assistant.** Customers browse the catalog, ask questions, place orders, and get a payment link — entirely inside WhatsApp. Order lands in the client's sheet/CRM/POS. *Buyers: local retail, food businesses, boutiques in WhatsApp-first markets.* Huge in regions where WhatsApp **is** the internet.

**A9. After-hours & overflow answering.** The cheaper cousin of A2: AI answers only when the line is busy or the office is closed — takes the message, books the slot, texts back missed calls instantly ("missed-call text-back"). *Buyers: trades, clinics, any small office.* Low price, painless yes, natural upgrade path to full A2.

## Category B — Marketing & growth (they bring your client customers)

**B1. Lead generation & enrichment.** Finds target companies, enriches contacts, scores them against the ideal-customer profile, delivers a clean list into the CRM weekly. *Buyers: B2B services, agencies, recruiters.*

**B2. Personalized outreach drafting.** Researches each prospect and drafts individual emails/LinkedIn messages in the client's voice — **draft-for-review, not auto-send** (deliverability + reputation + law). *Pairs with B1.*

**B3. Speed-to-lead follow-up.** New lead from any form/portal gets a personalized reply and booking link within 60 seconds, then a polite follow-up sequence until answer or opt-out. *Buyers: real-estate, insurance, education, anyone buying leads and wasting them.*

**B4. Content pipeline.** Turns raw inputs (product data, a weekly voice note, past posts) into on-brand social posts, product descriptions, and blog drafts queued for one-click approval. *Buyers: e-commerce, agencies (white-label), local brands.*

**B5. SEO content factory.** Keyword clusters → briefs → drafts → internal linking → publish queue, with human editorial approval. *Buyers: content-led businesses, agencies.* Higher monthly because volume is the product.

**B6. Ad copy & creative variants.** Generates and organizes ad copy/hook variants per audience, learns from performance exports which angles win. *Buyers: e-commerce, agencies.*

**B7. Newsletter automation.** Curates industry news + drafts the weekly newsletter in the client's voice for approval. *Buyers: consultants, B2B brands, communities.*

**B8. Video repurposing.** Long video/podcast in → clipped shorts suggestions, titles, captions, platform-ready posts out. *Buyers: coaches, podcasters, agencies.*

**B9. Social listening & brand monitoring.** Watches mentions, reviews, and competitor activity across platforms; daily digest with drafted responses where a reply is worth it. *Buyers: consumer brands, hospitality groups, agencies.*

**B10. AI product visuals.** Product photos on white and lifestyle backgrounds, seasonal banners, marketplace-format images — generated and upscaled from the client's raw shots. *Buyers: e-commerce, restaurants (menus), real-estate (staging).* Sold per batch or monthly.

**B11. Podcast production pipeline.** Raw recording in → cleaned audio notes, show notes, titles, episode article, clips list, distribution posts out. *Buyers: podcasters, coaches, B2B brands with a show.*

**B12. Translation & localization automation.** Product catalogs, support articles, and marketing content translated with glossary and tone control, human spot-check queue included. *Buyers: exporters, e-commerce entering new markets, tourism businesses.* Pairs with A6.

## Category C — Back office & documents (they save your client staff hours)

**C1. Invoice/receipt processing.** PDFs and photos → validated structured entries in the accounting tool; human review only for low-confidence items. *Buyers: accounting firms (volume!), logistics, property managers.*

**C2. Contract intake & summary.** Incoming contracts summarized, key terms extracted, risky clauses flagged for the lawyer's attention. Decision support, never legal advice. *Buyers: law firms, procurement, agencies.*

**C3. CV screening & recruitment pipeline.** CVs scored against the job spec with written reasoning, shortlist to the recruiter, rejection drafts prepared, interviews scheduled. *Buyers: recruitment agencies, HR departments.*

**C4. Email/form intake → CRM.** Every inquiry from any channel becomes a clean CRM record with source, intent, and next action. Kills copy-paste. *Everyone's second sale.*

**C5. Email triage & drafted replies.** The exec inbox sorted by urgency with reply drafts ready to edit-and-send. *Buyers: founders, partners, sales leads.* Very personal — high retention.

**C6. Meeting notes → actions.** Recordings/transcripts → summaries, task assignments in the PM tool, CRM updates. *Buyers: agencies, sales teams.*

**C7. Automated reporting.** Weekly KPI pack compiled from the client's tools into one branded PDF/dashboard with AI-written commentary. *Buyers: agencies reporting to their clients, managers reporting up.*

**C8. Expense categorization & bookkeeping prep.** Receipts and bank exports categorized, matched, and queued for the accountant with anomalies flagged. *Buyers: bookkeepers serving many SMBs, growing companies without a finance person.*

**C9. Payment reminders & collections.** Overdue invoices get polite, escalating, personalized reminders on schedule (email/WhatsApp), payment links included, human approval before the firm letters. *Buyers: any B2B with receivables pain — which is most of them.* Directly measurable ROI in recovered cash.

**C10. Proposal & quote generation.** A request email or call notes → a branded, priced proposal draft from the client's catalog and pricing rules, ready for review-and-send in minutes instead of days. *Buyers: agencies, contractors, B2B services.* Faster quotes win deals — easy story.

**C11. RFP / tender response drafting.** The RFP document parsed, requirements tabled, first-draft responses assembled from the client's past bids and capability library. Humans polish and submit. *Buyers: construction, IT services, facility management, anyone bidding for contracts.* High ticket — each won tender is worth thousands.

**C12. Compliance & policy Q&A.** Staff ask "can we do X?" and get grounded answers from the company's own policies and relevant regulations, with sources and an "ask legal" escape hatch. *Buyers: finance, healthcare-adjacent, franchises.*

## Category D — Internal AI, sales ops & custom (they make your client's team faster)

**D1. Workflow automation with AI steps.** The glue: any repetitive multi-tool process with a classify/summarize/draft step in the middle. Priced per workflow; sold in bundles. The universal door-opener alongside A1.

**D2. Internal knowledge assistant.** "ChatGPT that knows our company" — grounded answers over SOPs, policies, drive folders, with sources and access control. *Buyers: 20+ employee companies.*

**D3. SOP & documentation generator.** Watches how work is done (recordings, examples) and drafts the SOPs nobody ever writes. *Buyers: operations managers, franchises.*

**D4. Employee onboarding assistant.** New-hire Q&A bot + auto-generated onboarding checklists per role. *Buyers: growing SMBs, hospitality chains.*

**D5. Data analysis assistant.** Plain-language questions over the company's sheets/DB → charts + a written answer. Read-only by design. *Buyers: managers without analysts.*

**D6. Custom AI agents.** Multi-step autonomous flows with human checkpoints: claim intake, order orchestration, complex intake flows. Sold only to existing clients (trust first). Top of the price list.

**D7. CRM sales copilot.** Every deal gets an AI-maintained summary, next-step suggestions, and drafted follow-ups inside the CRM the team already uses. *Buyers: sales teams of 3–20 reps.* Sticky — it lives where they work.

**D8. Managed AI SDR.** You run the client's entire outbound engine as a service: list building (B1) + personalized drafting (B2) + sending infrastructure + reply triage + booked meetings on their calendar. Compliant sending only (warm-up, volume caps, opt-out, no spam-law violations). *Buyers: B2B companies without sales staff.* Highest monthly on the menu — it replaces a hire.

**D9. Forecasting & anomaly alerts.** Watches sales, stock, or bookings data and messages the owner when something drifts: "stock-out risk on X in 12 days", "bookings 30% below normal week". *Buyers: retail, e-commerce, hospitality groups.*

**D10. Website + built-in AI assistant.** A modern one-page or small business site delivered **with** an A1 chatbot and booking flow built in from day one. *Buyers: new businesses, businesses with 2010-era websites.* Bundling the site makes you the whole answer, not an add-on.

## Category F — Consulting, training, subscriptions & products (revenue beyond building)

**F1. AI audit.** Paid assessment: map the client's processes, find the top 3 automation opportunities, deliver a roadmap with ROI estimates. Pays you to do sales discovery; fee credited against the first build.

**F2. AI strategy retainer.** A monthly advisor seat for companies that build internally but need direction: tool choices, vendor review, prompt/eval practices, hiring input. *Sells after you have case studies.*

**F3. Corporate AI training.** Half-day/full-day workshops: "AI for your team's daily work" (generic) or role-specific (AI for recruiters, for agents, for accountants). Per-day pricing, materials reused. Converts to builds.

**F4. Automation rescue.** Fix the broken chatbot / half-finished Zapier project / abandoned GPT-wrapper another vendor left behind. Diagnosis fee + repair quote. *Endless supply; instant hero status; converts to retainer.*

**F5. White-label delivery.** Web/marketing agencies sell "AI services" — you deliver under their brand at 60–70% of list. One good agency = recurring deal flow without marketing cost.

**F6. Automation blueprints.** From Phase 4: documented n8n workflow templates + prompt sets + setup guides ($50–500) sold to DIY buyers and other freelancers. Products from work already done.

**F7. AI Employee subscription plans.** The catalog re-packaged as **flat monthly "digital workers"** — the easiest thing a small business has ever bought:
| Plan | What the "employee" does | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Receptionist | A9 or A2-lite + A5 + A7 | $390–590/mo + setup |
| Digital Marketing Assistant | B4 + B7 + B10 (quota-based) | $490–790/mo + setup |
| Digital Back Office | C4 + C6 + C9 (quota-based) | $590–990/mo + setup |

One invoice, no project scoping, cancel with 30 days notice, quotas keep usage sane. **This is the productized retainer that scales past custom work** — introduced in Phase 2–3 ([07](07-scaling-roadmap.md)).

**F8. Affiliate & reseller commissions.** The tools you deploy anyway (hosting, n8n cloud, voice platforms, CRMs) pay 10–30% recurring referral commissions. Sign up for partner programs once; every client you onboard adds passive monthly income. Rule: only recommend what you'd deploy regardless — trust is worth more than any commission.

**F9. Course & community.** From Phase 4, teach what you do: a "build AI automations for local businesses" course ($50–300) and/or a paid community/membership ($20–50/mo) for other builders — who then also buy your blueprints (F6) and become your certified-setup network. Authority marketing that pays for itself.

## What we do NOT do (unchanged, and it protects the whole catalog)

- No custom model training / fine-tuning projects — prompt + RAG solves 95% of SMB cases at 5% of the cost.
- No "build me an AI startup" product engineering.
- No fully autonomous actions on money, legal, or medical decisions — AI drafts, humans approve ([Operations & Legal](05-operations-legal.md)).
- No scraping or outreach that violates platform terms or spam laws; outbound voice and managed SDR always disclose, cap volume, and honor opt-out.
- No unlimited free changes — scope is written; changes are welcome and billed.
