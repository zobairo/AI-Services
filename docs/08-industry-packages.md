# Industry Packages

Bundles of catalog services ([02](02-services-catalog.md)) wrapped in one pitch, one price, one onboarding — per niche. Packages are how you escape "custom quote every time": easier to sell ("this is what clinics get"), easier to deliver (same template stack), easier to price up (bundle value > sum of parts to the client, while your delivery cost per service drops).

Pricing shows **setup + monthly**. Setup ranges: lower end while building reputation, upper end established. Monthly includes hosting, monitoring, the retainer SLA (Growth tier by default — [05](05-operations-legal.md)), and an agreed usage allowance; voice minutes billed as usage beyond allowance.

## E6 — Agency White-Label Program — "Your AI department, our delivery"

*For: web/marketing agencies whose clients keep asking for AI.*

- Any package above delivered under the agency's brand at **60–70% of list price**; agency sets the client price.
- You provide: delivery, hosting, monitoring, second-line support, sales-call support as "their AI team".
- Agency provides: the client relationship, first-line contact, invoicing to their client.
- Terms: minimum 2 client projects/quarter to keep program status; NDA + non-solicitation both ways ([05](05-operations-legal.md)).

One active agency ≈ 4–10 delivered packages/year with zero marketing cost. Target: 2–3 active agencies by Phase 3 ([07](07-scaling-roadmap.md)).

## Package rules

1. **Fixed contents.** Swaps allowed only within category and same size ("no product finder — abandoned-cart flow instead"). Custom = leave the package, quote as custom (higher).
2. **Package price ≈ 15–25% below** the sum of individual list prices — real bundle discount, still better margin via shared templates and one onboarding.
3. **Every package ships with the standard acceptance test cases** per included service — delivery quality doesn't loosen because it's a bundle.
4. **One-pager per package** (outcome headline, what's included, price, 2 case-study numbers) — this is the sales asset in [GTM](04-go-to-market.md).
5. **Retire or fix any package that hasn't sold in 2 quarters.** The lineup earns its place with revenue.
