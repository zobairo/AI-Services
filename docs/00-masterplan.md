# The Masterplan

One page that holds the whole picture. Every other document is a zoom-in on part of this.

## Vision

Build an **AI automation company** in four stages: start as a solo service provider, become a boutique agency with contractors, then productize the best-selling work into packages and digital products, and finally spin the most-demanded automation into a standalone product. Services fund the journey; templates and products compound it.

**Mission sentence for clients:** *"We give businesses AI employees — systems that answer, write, process, and follow up 24/7 — for less than the cost of one part-time hire."*

## The eight revenue streams

| # | Stream | What it is | Starts in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Build fees** | Fixed-price automation projects ($300 – $12,000 per project) | Phase 1 |
| 2 | **Retainers (MRR)** | Monthly hosting, monitoring, improvements ($100 – $800/mo per client) | Phase 1 |
| 3 | **Usage margins** | LLM tokens & voice minutes billed at cost + 20–50% | Phase 1 |
| 4 | **Consulting & training** | Paid AI audits, strategy retainers, corporate workshops | Phase 2 |
| 5 | **White-label & partners** | Delivery for web/marketing agencies at 60–70% of list price | Phase 2 |
| 6 | **AI Employee subscriptions** | Flat-fee "digital workers" ($390 – $990/mo plans) — productized retainers | Phase 2–3 |
| 7 | **Affiliate & reseller** | 10–30% recurring commissions on tools you deploy anyway | Phase 3 |
| 8 | **Digital products & education** | Blueprints ($50–500), course/community, then a SaaS spin-off | Phase 3–4 |

## The five phases

| Phase | When | What happens | Exit target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Solo provider** | Months 0–3 | Niche chosen, stack live, sell 2 lead services, first clients, first retainers | $2–5k/mo run rate, 3–5 retainers · [90-day roadmap](06-roadmap-90-days.md) |
| **2 — Full catalog** | Months 4–9 | Expand service menu category by category, add voice agents + training + white-label + first AI-Employee plan, hire a VA | $8–12k/mo, $3–5k MRR |
| **3 — Boutique agency** | Months 10–18 | 1–2 contractor builders, industry packages as the main offer, managed AI SDR + subscriptions, you move to sales + architecture | $15–25k/mo, $8–12k MRR |
| **4 — Productize** | Months 18–24 | Blueprint/template sales, course & community, partner network, pick the SaaS spin-off candidate from real demand data | Products ≥ 20% of revenue |
| **5 — Scale or spin off** | Months 24–36 | Choose the year-3 path with data: grow the agency, go all-in on the product, or hold a lean profitable boutique | $25–60k/mo depending on path |

## Operating principles (the rules that don't change)

1. **Sell outcomes, not technology.** Nobody buys workflows; they buy answered customers and saved hours.
2. **Retainers over projects.** Every delivery ends with a retainer pitch. MRR is the business.
3. **Template everything.** Every project becomes a reusable blueprint. Project #10 takes 30% of the time of project #1.
4. **Cheapest model that passes the tests.** Margin lives in model tiering and prompt caching.
5. **Human-in-the-loop on anything irreversible.** Trust is the product; one bad hallucination incident costs more than ten clients bring.
6. **Outreach every day**, including delivery weeks. The pipeline never sleeps.
7. **Raise prices every 2–3 wins.** If nobody says "too expensive," you're too cheap.
8. **No client over 40% of revenue.** Concentration is fragility.
