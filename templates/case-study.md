# Case Study Template

The asset you traded your first low price for ([docs/04](../docs/04-go-to-market.md)). One page, real numbers, published on your site and attached to every proposal after it.

Collect the numbers at **day 30 after go-live** — early enough that the client still remembers the pain, late enough that the results are real.

---

*Notes for you (delete before publishing):*

- **Get written permission** before publishing anything with their name in it. If they say no, publish it anonymised: "a 3-dentist clinic in the north of England" still sells.
- **Do not round numbers up.** 78% is more believable than 80%, and you will be asked to prove it on a sales call.
- **Where the numbers come from:** the conversation logs your service writes, the [monthly report](monthly-report.md) you already send them, and one question at day 30: "roughly how many hours a week has this saved you?"
- **Get three things at day 30, in one email:** these numbers, a written testimonial, and the referral introduction they agreed to at the start.
