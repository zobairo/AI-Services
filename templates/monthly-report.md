# Monthly Client Report Template

Fifteen minutes of your time, and the single most effective churn-prevention tool you have ([docs/05](../docs/05-operations-legal.md), [docs/09](../docs/09-financial-projections.md)). Send it on the same day each month. Never skip it, even in a quiet month — the month you stop reporting is the month they start wondering what they pay for.
