## Upwork / freelance profile

**Title** (this is 80% of whether anyone clicks):

> AI Automation for Small Business — Chatbots, WhatsApp Assistants, Document Processing

Not "AI Engineer". Not "Full-Stack Developer". Clients search for the thing they want done.

**Overview** — first two lines are all most people read:

> I build AI assistants that answer your customers 24/7 and automations that stop your team retyping the same data. Working demo before you commit to anything.
>
> What I build most:
> • Support chatbots for websites and WhatsApp, answering from your own prices and documents
> • Invoice and document processing — PDFs in, clean data in your accounting tool
> • Instant lead follow-up — every enquiry answered in under a minute, day or night
> • Workflow automation connecting the tools you already use
>
> How I work: I build a demo on your real content first, so you see it working before you pay the balance. Every project has a written list of test questions it must pass before I call it delivered. Your data is never used to train AI models.
>
> I say no to projects I do not think will pay for themselves. If a spreadsheet solves it, I will tell you.
>
> Recent: a dental clinic went from missing 30 calls a week to zero. A supplier-invoice automation cut data entry from 9 hours a week to under 1.

**Skills to list:** AI Chatbot, Chatbot Development, n8n, Automation, API Integration, WhatsApp API, Document Processing, Data Extraction, Workflow Automation, Anthropic Claude.

**Rate:** start at $30–45/hour equivalent, but **quote fixed prices per project** ([docs/01](../docs/01-business-model-and-pricing.md)). Raise it after every 2–3 five-star reviews.

**Portfolio items** — three entries, each with a video, the problem, what you built, and the result. Use the demos below until real client work replaces them.

## The two demo videos

Both under three minutes. Screen recording, your voice, no slides, no intro animation. Record them once and send them for months.

### Video 1 — the customer assistant (2:30)

| Time | What you show | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | The chat widget, nothing typed yet | "This is a customer assistant for a dental clinic. Everything it knows comes from the clinic's own price list and policies." |
| 0:15–0:50 | Ask "how much is a cleaning?" then "do you take Delta Dental?" | "Real prices, real insurers, straight from their documents — and it shows which document each answer came from." |
| 0:50–1:30 | Ask "what is the capital of France?" → it hands off | "This is the important part. When it does not know, it does not guess. It passes the customer to a human." |
| 1:30–2:05 | Ask "I have bad tooth pain, what painkiller should I take?" → hands off | "It will not give medical advice, even though it has a page about treatments. Those rules are set per business." |
| 2:05–2:30 | Terminal: `npm test`, tests passing | "Before it goes live, it has to pass a list of real questions the business approves. That is what 'finished' means." |

### Video 2 — document processing (2:00)

| Time | What you show | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | The folder of invoices | "Six supplier documents — different layouts, one in French, one a bad scan." |
| 0:20–1:00 | Run it, results appear | "Four went straight through with the right totals. It checks that subtotal plus tax equals the total — if the numbers do not reconcile, nobody posts it." |
| 1:00–1:30 | The review queue entry | "The bad scan is not silently half-entered. It is on a list with the reason attached." |
| 1:30–2:00 | The delivery note being rejected | "And this one is a delivery note, not an invoice. Same supplier, same goods. It never reaches the accounts." |

**Recording tips:** speak slower than feels natural, do one take and keep the small mistakes (it reads as real), and end with "if you want to see this on your own prices, send me your website — I will build it and send it back."

## Case study placement

Once you have one real client result ([case study template](../templates/case-study.md)), it goes in four places the same day: your site's demo section, your Upwork portfolio, the proposal template's footer, and touch 2 of the [follow-up sequence](outreach-scripts.md).

---

## What to skip at the start

Logo design, a company name you agonise over, a multi-page website, business cards, an LLC before you have revenue, a personal brand strategy. None of these has ever won a first client. The demo video does.
