# Appointments

FICTIONAL DEMO DATA.

## How to book an appointment

You can book:

- On WhatsApp or by phone: +1 555 0134
- By email: hello@northsidedental.example
- Through the online booking page on our website, which shows live availability

When you book we ask for your name, date of birth, phone number, and whether you are a new or existing patient. New patients get a medical history form by email to complete before the visit.

Routine check-ups are usually available within 5 to 7 days. Hygienist appointments are typically booked 2 to 3 weeks ahead, so book the next one before you leave the clinic.

## How to cancel or reschedule

Please give us at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or move your appointment. Call, message us on WhatsApp, or use the link in your reminder message.

If you cancel with less than 24 hours notice, or do not arrive, we charge a late cancellation fee of $40 ($75 for appointments longer than an hour, such as implant or orthodontic visits). The fee is waived once for genuine emergencies and illness — just tell us what happened.

## Appointment reminders

We send a reminder by WhatsApp or SMS 48 hours before your appointment, and a second one on the morning of the visit. You can reply to that message to confirm, move or cancel. If you would rather not get reminders, tell reception and we will switch them off for your record.

## Running late

If you are running late, message us on WhatsApp. We can usually still see you if you arrive within 10 minutes. Later than that and we may need to shorten or move the appointment, because the next patient is already waiting.

## Waiting list for earlier slots

Ask reception to add you to the cancellation list and we will message you when an earlier slot opens up. Most people on the list get offered something within two weeks.

## Dental emergencies

If you are in pain, have a knocked-out tooth, swelling, or bleeding that will not stop, call +1 555 0134 during opening hours and say it is an emergency — we keep slots free every day for this.

Outside opening hours call our emergency line on +1 555 0199. For a knocked-out adult tooth, keep the tooth in milk and come in immediately: it can often be saved within the first hour. If you have severe facial swelling, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or a serious injury, go to your nearest hospital emergency department instead.

## First visit — what to bring

Bring a list of any medicines you take, your insurance details if you have cover, and photo ID for your first visit. If you have X-rays from another dentist taken in the last 12 months, email them to us beforehand and we may not need to repeat them.
