# Payment and Insurance

FICTIONAL DEMO DATA.

## How you can pay

We accept cash, all major debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and bank transfer. Payment is taken at the end of each visit. For treatment over $1,000 we ask for a 30% deposit when the work is booked, and the balance on the day the work is finished.

We do not take payment over the phone from anyone who calls claiming to be from the clinic — if you ever get a call like that, hang up and phone us on +1 555 0134.

## Payment plans and instalments

Treatment over $800 can be split into monthly instalments:

- 3 months, interest free, no credit check
- 6 or 12 months through our finance partner, subject to approval
- Orthodontic treatment can be spread across the whole treatment length

Ask reception for a written plan showing the monthly amount before you decide. There is no charge for the interest-free option.

## Insurance we work with

We are in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna Dental and MetLife. We also treat patients with Aetna and Guardian cover as an out-of-network provider, which usually means you pay us and claim the money back.

Bring your insurance card or policy number to your first visit and we will check your cover before treatment starts. We submit in-network claims for you directly; for out-of-network plans we give you a fully itemised receipt to send to your insurer.

## What insurance usually covers

Most plans cover check-ups and cleaning at 80-100%, fillings and extractions at around 50-80%, and major work like crowns and implants at 0-50%. Cosmetic treatment such as whitening and veneers is almost never covered.

Every policy is different, so we always confirm your actual cover with your insurer before major work — this takes 2 to 5 working days and we tell you the exact amount you will pay before booking.

## Estimates and receipts

You get a written estimate before any treatment beyond a check-up, and an itemised receipt after every visit by email. If you need a receipt again for tax or insurance, message us and we will resend it.

## Help with cost

If cost is a worry, tell us. We can often stage treatment over several months, start with the urgent work only, or offer a lower-cost alternative — for example a filling or a well-made denture instead of an implant. We would rather find a plan you can afford than have you avoid the dentist.
