## Opening hours

We are open:

- Monday to Thursday: 08:30 - 18:00
- Friday: 08:30 - 15:00
- Saturday: 09:00 - 13:00 (hygiene and check-up appointments only)
- Sunday: closed

The clinic is closed on public holidays. The last appointment of each day starts 45 minutes before closing time.

## Address and parking

Northside Dental Clinic is at 118 Harbour Road, Northside, on the ground floor of the Harbour Medical Building.

There is free patient parking for 12 cars behind the building — enter from Mill Lane. Street parking on Harbour Road is paid until 18:00. The number 7 and number 22 buses stop directly outside, and Northside train station is a 6 minute walk away.

The clinic is fully wheelchair accessible with a step-free entrance and an accessible bathroom on the same floor.

## New patients

We are accepting new patients. Your first visit is a full check-up with X-rays, a discussion of any concerns, and a written treatment plan with prices before anything is booked. Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete a medical history form, or fill it in online from the link we send when you book.

## Children and families

We treat children from age 2. Check-ups for children under 12 are free when a parent is a registered patient at the clinic. We have a family room with space for pushchairs and a play corner.
